Seven years vs four months — Maresca before Arsenal 🧩 Swipe ➡️

Man City won their first seven league games under Enzo Maresca. Now they are ten games without a clean sheet and six points behind leaders Arsenal before Sunday's trip to the Emirates.

On the defence: "We went from four or five clean sheets in a row, now we are not able to keep clean sheets."

On the slump: "I'm not a magician. There is transition, it's normal." And: "I'm completely relaxed, no panic at all, I understand that we need to win games because it's our business."

On Arteta's Arsenal: "For me, it's seven years together against four months together so you cannot compare."

On Erling Haaland: "It's normal that he's not happy when he's not winning games but don't forget a few weeks ago he was dancing inside the pitch." And: "I don't see any problem at all with Erling."

Fair point from Maresca — or should City already be closer to Arsenal? 👇
