Only 1-0 at half time... how many by full time? 🇩🇪 Germany hit the Netherlands for five in Berlin.

Derry Scherhant hits the post, then scores just before the break from Joshua Kimmich's pass. Marc-André ter Stegen keeps out Cody Gakpo, and Florian Wirtz takes over: 2-0, then Nico Schlotterbeck heads in Brajan Gruda's ball and Wirtz gets his second from another Gruda assist. Gruda pounces for the fifth after Scherhant hits the post again. Germany, the Netherlands and Greece all finish on 11 points, and Germany come out on top to reach the quarter-finals.
