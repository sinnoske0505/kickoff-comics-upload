Magazine-style cover: Bournemouth substitute Rayan roars in celebration in a red-and-black striped shirt; headline "Down 1-0. Down to 10. Won 2-1."
Panel 1: The Bournemouth fox cheers in the away end at Balaídos as Celta Vigo and Bournemouth walk out; Bournemouth had won all four of their European games.
Panel 2, 19 minutes: Iago Aspas cuts inside and curls a left-footed shot into the corner, 1-0 Celta.
Panel 3, 23 minutes: After a VAR review, Bafodé Diakité's yellow card for a late tackle on Couhaib Driouech becomes a red.
Panel 4, first half: Celta think they have scored again, but the assistant referee raises the offside flag.
Panel 5, 47 minutes: Álvaro Rodríguez glances in Amine Adli's cross at the far post, 1-1.
Panel 6, second half: Michele Di Gregorio stretches to keep out another Aspas curler.
Panel 7, 78 minutes: Substitute Rayan cuts inside and slots the ball under the keeper after a Truffert break and Tavernier pass, 1-2.
Panel 8, full time: Ten-man Bournemouth celebrate a 10th straight win, the first English club to win its first five games in major European competition, and the fox leaps in the stands.
