"I'm ready." 🎙️ Viktor Gyökeres before Arsenal vs Man City, in 8 panels ➡️

Gyökeres scored early in Tuesday's 1-1 with Dortmund. On the chances Arsenal missed: "It's just a matter of time."
Asked about not starting every league game: "I just prepare myself and I'm ready."

On City, who have taken one point from their last four league games: "They still have a very good squad, very good players." A bad run, he said, is "normal for any club."

City are appealing September's guilty verdict over Premier League financial rules (they deny wrongdoing; no sanction has been set). Gyökeres: "It is what it is. We can't control that. It has nothing to do with us."

And the aim: "We always want to win titles." Arsenal, the defending champions, host City on Sunday six points clear at the top.
