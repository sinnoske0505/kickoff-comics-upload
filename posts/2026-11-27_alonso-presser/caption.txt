Xabi Alonso on Chelsea's "fragile moment" 🧩 Swipe ➡️

Chelsea were beaten 4-1 by Leeds at Stamford Bridge on Saturday, Leeds' first win there since 1999. It followed a 1-0 defeat at Sunderland: two league losses in a row.

Ahead of Friday's trip to Nottingham Forest, Alonso didn't hide from it. "I spoke about fragile moment... we want to show our reaction." Asked about the owners: "The focus has been tomorrow." And on what needs fixing: "basics in football that are so important to compete at this level."

On Wesley Fofana's unauthorised YouTube interview: "It was unfortunate. He's apologized." Is the case closed? "Closed."

And on dealing with physical strikers: "That's the standards of the Premier League."
