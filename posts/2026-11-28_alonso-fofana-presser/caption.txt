"We need to stick together" — Alonso after Forest 🧩 Swipe ➡️

Nottingham Forest 3-2 Chelsea on Friday night. Before kick-off, Xabi Alonso said: "Today we need strong characters, big personalities."

Wesley Fofana was booed by Chelsea's own supporters, brought down Liam Delap for the penalty that made it 2-0, and was later substituted. Chelsea have now lost four of their last five league games.

Afterwards, Alonso admitted: "We are going through a difficult moment in solidity, in confidence."

Asked about the fans booing Fofana: "We need to support everyone because we need everyone. We need the fans, we need all the players, we need to stick together, because if not, it will get more complicated."

On the way out of it: "No one will come from outside to give us a solution… it needs to come from within the team." And: "In football, this can change quickly, or this can continue."
