"It's to blame us" — Pochettino on Lammens 🧤 Swipe through ➡️

Sporting 2-2 Man Utd in the Champions League. Senne Lammens saved a Luis Suarez penalty, gave away another 75 seconds later, then spilled the ball for Suarez's 80th-minute equaliser — after Bruno Fernandes had scored twice to put United 2-1 up. United also had two goals ruled out on the night.

Mauricio Pochettino, still waiting for his first win as United head coach, didn't point at his keeper: "That is football. Football is about making mistakes. I always want to support my players."

He said the defence must "cover the keeper better" — while stressing "it's not to blame the defensive line" — and added: "It's to blame us… when you are a keeper, is not easy and you always need help from your teammates."
