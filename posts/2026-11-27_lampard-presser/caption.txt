Coventry visit Leeds this weekend, and Frank Lampard knows the history. "I played for Chelsea for many years and Chelsea and Leeds have a very strong rivalry," he said.

Then there's Derby. In January 2019, Leeds admitted a staff member had watched a Derby training session while Lampard was in charge. His take now: "At Derby we had public matters which are all history for me but some people do hold onto them."

And then there's Dominic Calvert-Lewin, whose 85th-minute header completed Everton's comeback from 2-0 down against Crystal Palace in May 2022 and kept Lampard's side up. "He gave me one of my best moments certainly in managerial life." Now he leads the line for Leeds: "one of the best and most dangerous strikers in the league."

Old friends, new rivals. Who comes out on top? 👇
