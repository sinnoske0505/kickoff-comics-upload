The day before Man City 2-2 Napoli, Kevin De Bruyne faced the media at his old home.

On his future in Naples: "There's not a lot I can say for the moment."
On reports in Italy: "It's not been nice for my wife or my family."
In September, Man City were reported to have been found guilty on almost all of the Premier League's 115 charges. (reports: Al Jazeera, FourFourTwo). Possible sanctions, including titles, have not been finalised. Asked about his old club: "I'm proud of what I've done... this is my club, you know. I love the owners. I have nothing but affection for this club and that will never change."
On City possibly losing titles: "It still doesn't change. My football has not changed... I played in between the white lines and I competed with other players."
