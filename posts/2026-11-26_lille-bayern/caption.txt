Bayern went ahead… then Lille scored twice in three minutes 😳 Lille 2-1 Bayern in 8 panels ➡️

Without Harry Kane and Michael Olise, Bayern led on 15 minutes when Dayot Upamecano headed in Tom Bischof's corner at the back post.

Bayern had around 65% of the ball, but Lille kept defending and Berke Ozer kept making saves. On 62 minutes Nathan Ngoy slipped Ayase Ueda through and he finished low into the far corner. Three minutes later Ethan Mbappe hit the post and Aleksandar Pavlovic turned the rebound into his own net.

Soriba Diaoune could have made it 3-1 late on. It's Lille's first win of this league phase, and Bayern's first Champions League defeat of the season.
